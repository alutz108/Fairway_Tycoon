Build courses, run your clubs and grow a golf empire. New land goes up for sale as your empire grows.

How to build

  1. Tap 🏗️ Build, pick Hole, tap where the tee goes, then tap where the pin goes. A green is built around the pin.
  2. Want the clubhouse somewhere else? Use 🏗️ Build → 🏠 Clubhouse. It’s free to move until you build your first hole.
  3. Paint fairway between them so golfers have somewhere good to land.
  4. Put bunkers, trees and ponds near the landing areas. Golfers love a hole with some danger.
  5. Press ▶ to open. Golfers pay the green fee, play your holes and tell you what they think.

Holes under 250 yards are par 3, up to 470 par 4, longer par 5. A mix of pars raises your rating.
