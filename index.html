Pick a setting, design your course, and run the club. Every setting has an optional challenge if you want a goal to chase.

How to build

  1. Pick Hole, tap where the tee goes, then tap where the pin goes. A green is built around the pin.
  2. Paint fairway between them so golfers have somewhere good to land.
  3. Put bunkers, trees and ponds near the landing areas. Golfers love a hole with some danger.
  4. Press ▶ to open. Golfers pay the green fee, play your holes and tell you what they think.

Holes under 250 yards are par 3, up to 470 par 4, longer par 5. A mix of pars raises your rating.
